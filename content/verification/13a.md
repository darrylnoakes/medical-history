## imatinib: corrected
- Body said "More than 70 similar kinase-blocking drugs". The source counts all small-molecule kinase inhibitors (protein and lipid kinases, cancer and non-cancer), not "similar" drugs. Reworded.
- Confirmed: Nowell and Hungerford 1960, Hungerford as graduate student, BCR-ABL, Druker and Lydon, ten-week FDA review (approval was for interferon-failure patients first). Doubt: the "more than 70" count is from 2021 and is now higher.

## sars-2003: corrected
- Summary said the virus "jumped continents by plane" and was a "coronavirus"; no cited source says either. Replaced with wording the WHO table supports (China, Vietnam, Canada).
- "Governments could hide outbreaks, as China at first did" overstated the Baker and Fidler wording ("China's initial fears that disclosing... would harm its economy"). Softened.
- Confirmed: 8,096 cases, 774 deaths, 1,706 health workers, first onset 16 November 2002, Urbani dates, IHR adopted May 2005.
