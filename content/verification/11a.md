## nuremberg-code: corrected
- Body said the 1931 German guidelines "were never repealed". Ghooi says only that they "remained in force until 1948" and were not carried into the postwar omnibus law. Changed to "still in force".
- Body said the syphilis study ran 25 more years "with no sign that anyone weighed it against the code". Tobin says "no evidence" it was ever discussed in light of the code. Wording now follows the source.
- Body said Helsinki drew "heavily" on the code. The 2024 review says it was based on the code "and mainly" on a 1954 WMA document. Changed to say both.
- Myth record said "binding rules came only after Helsinki". Helsinki is not binding. Now cites US regulation in the 1970s (1974 rules, supported by the Ochsner article).
- Date display narrowed from "August 1947" to 19 August 1947 (Harvard's timeline: tribunal judgment 19 Aug, sentences 20 Aug).
- Link to `eugenics` was marked `built-on`, which is wrong (the code does not draw on eugenics) and its claim was unsourced. Now `see-also` with a modest note. Link note to `henrietta-lacks` made precise.
- Confirmed: 23 defendants, 9 December 1946 opening, 16 convicted, 7 executed, 7 acquitted, Alexander's memorandum of 17 April 1947 (six points expanded to ten), "Permissible Medical Experiments", Beecher's 22 studies, 1974 commission, 1979 Belmont Report. Image (Karl Brandt sentenced; US National Archives, public domain) matches the file title and metadata.
- Residual doubt: authorship of the ten points (judges versus Ivy and Alexander) and the debt to the 1931 guidelines rest on one critical article (Ghooi); the entry already flags this.
