# Verification report, batch 10b

Second-pass fact-check of nine entries. All sources were re-read from the saved copies of the cited pages (or re-fetched); Commons file pages were checked for all five images.

## influenza-1918 (tier 1) — corrected (minor)
- All figures (three waves, first wave best described in the US in March 1918, case fatality above 2.5% versus below 0.1%, about 500 million infected, W-shaped curve at ages 20–40, about 50 and up to 100 million deaths, descendants of the 1918 virus, Spain's neutral press, Barry's Haskell County, Oxford's French army camp in 1916, China) were found in the cited sources.
- Spreeuwenberg et al. quote was a truncated fragment that did not carry the 17.4 million figure; replaced with the abstract sentence that says the death toll was "not as severe as most frequently cited estimates" (the abstract gives n = 17.4 million).
- Image caption said the 1918 photo showed a camp "hit in the pandemic's first wave"; Commons dates the photo only "circa 1918", so the caption now says "about 1918" and drops the wave claim.
- Residual doubt: the "bacterial pneumonia" statement rests on a source saying secondary bacterial infections were found in nearly all fatal cases examined; the exact share of deaths is not established.

## insulin (tier 1) — corrected
- Charles Best was called "medical student"; the University of Toronto page says he graduated in physiology and biochemistry in spring 1921. Changed to "recent physiology graduate", added life dates (1899–1978) and added that page as a source.
- Body said Macleod "brought in" Collip; the sources say only that Collip "joined" in December 1921 (Li: on sabbatical from Alberta). Reworded.
- "Pancreine" changed to "pancreina", the spelling in the source.
- Paulescu was described as "a leading antisemitic agitator"; the cited paper says he took part in creating far-right antisemitic organizations. Reworded to that, and the 2003 cancellation now says it followed an accusation by the Simon Wiesenthal Center (per the same paper).
- Residual doubt: Leonard Thompson's age is given as 13 by Li and 14 by the University of Toronto; the body avoids stating it. Credit for the purification remains argued over.
