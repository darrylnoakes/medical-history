## dioscorides: corrected
- Body and myth put "a soldier's life" in quotation marks; Riddle's source gives "soldier-like life" (and a garbled "soldier' life"). Changed to the sourced wording.
- Date confidence note said "a contemporary who names him"; Riddle says Erotian mentions him only "assuming the name is not an interpolation". Caveat added; Galen's later use added as the other dating clue.
- Body said "written ... around 50-70 CE"; Riddle gives "fl. 50-70". Reworded to "probably between about 50 and 70".
- Cut "where each substance grows" (not in sources). Myth record no longer implies army service is based only on the phrase (Riddle also cites his travels and his time with Areius), and notes the Nero link is a later inference.
- Summary "standard drug book" softened to "a leading drug authority"; Galen link note was unsupported ("added drugs he had not described") and was replaced.
